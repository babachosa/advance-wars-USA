RA SOAT TEXT ROM ADVANCE WARS 1 (USA)
====================================

ROM: 4.194.304 byte; SHA-1 D0A0A4CFE9B95AC7118F7EF476F014CA0242EB65.

Ban CSV cu co 2701 chuoi. Quet lai toan ROM cho thay no chua du:
- 3 mau hoi thoai ngan tai 0x2FA190, 0x2FCA14, 0x2FDAAC.
- Ten dia hinh va ten phe trong giao dien/multiplayer.
- 53 chuoi Shift-JIS tieng Nhat trong payload multiplayer.
- Mot so nhan debug/noi bo va chuoi dinh dang.

Da bo sung 128 dong, tong cong 2829 chuoi truc tiep. Toan bo 2701 dong
CSV cu duoc giu nguyen byte, gom ca ban dich. Trong 128 dong moi, 73 dong
da duoc dien ban dich/ten thich hop; 55 dong ten rieng, debug hoac ma ky
thuat duoc de nguyen. Tool "check" thanh cong: 2774 dong co ban dich,
55 dong giu nguyen.

Lenh "python aw_vi_tool.py audit" quet 4 MiB va hien 2 mau chu con
lai ngoai CSV: 0x071895 "CTF CLF CDF C" va 0x31E087 "UtthftF XX ".
Ca hai nam trong du lieu nhi phan, khong phai text co nghia.
Thu giai nen 703 khoi LZ77 hop le khong tim them cum tu game ro rang.
